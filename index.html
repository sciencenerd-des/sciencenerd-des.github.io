Late deliveries and one-star reviews

Public Olist e-commerce dataset, 99,441 orders

Problem

An online seller wants to know what drives bad reviews, and whether delivery is worth fixing first.

Approach

Cleaned the order, customer and review tables, matched promised and actual delivery dates, and compared review scores for late and on-time orders by state and month.

Outcome

Late orders averaged 2.27 stars against 4.29 on time, and 54% of late orders got one star, against 7% on time. Only 3% of customers ordered again, so a late first order rarely gets a second chance.

Manufacturing costing and quote workbook

Excel template with sample products, no client data

Problem

A small manufacturer needs to price a quote from the real cost of a batch, not a rough markup.

Approach

One workbook with 13 linked sheets: suppliers, bill of materials, labour, machine hours, tooling, quality, packaging and logistics roll into a cost summary with overhead allocation.

Outcome

Pick a product and the workbook shows unit cost, selling price, gross margin and each cost's share, then fills the customer quote. No macros, so it opens in any copy of Excel.

GST invoice checks and 2B reconciliation

Prototype on synthetic invoices, not a production system

Problem

Accountants check GST invoices by hand: the GSTIN, the line maths, the CGST and SGST split, and whether purchases match GSTR-2B.

Approach

An AI model reads the invoice photo into fixed fields. Plain Python rules then make every correctness call: GSTIN checksum, line totals, tax split, and the 2B against purchase-register match.

Outcome

A working demo that flags bad GSTINs and totals that don't add up, and runs offline with no API key. Results still need a person's review before any filing decision.

Which acquisition channels pay back

Simulated Indian e-commerce market; channel costs are assumptions

Problem

A growth team needs to compare what each channel costs to acquire a customer with what that customer is worth.

Approach

A Python pipeline builds a star schema (25,001 sessions, 8,443 customers), then funnel, cohort retention and CAC against LTV views, with a Power BI data model and DAX alongside.

Outcome

A dashboard where the channel costs are editable, so the team can test "what if" spend before committing budget. Every figure traces back to the processed tables.

Questions clients ask

If yours isn't here, ask it in your first email.

Should I use Excel, Power BI or Tableau?

Usually whatever your team already opens every day. Excel suits small teams and anything you'll print or email. Power BI suits teams on Microsoft 365 who want shared, refreshable reports. Tableau suits richer exploration. I'll recommend one on the scoping call and explain why.

What do you need from me to quote?

A sample of the data (a few months of exports is enough), the questions the report should answer, who will use it, and your deadline. I reply with follow-up questions or a fixed price and scope.

My data comes from Tally, Zoho Books, Shopify or a marketplace. Can you work with it?

If it exports to Excel or CSV, yes. I'll set up the cleaning steps so next month's export drops in without redoing the work.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes, for the written scope. If you ask for something outside it, I'll quote that change separately before doing it, so there are no surprise bills.

How long does a project take?

A single dashboard takes about two weeks, with a working draft by day five. A cleanup or audit is often a few days. Larger multi-page work gets a dated plan in the scope.

Can I hire you through Freelancer or Upwork?

Yes. You can hire me directly by email, or through Freelancer or Upwork if you prefer the platform's payment protection. The scope and process are the same either way.

Do you build AI agents or automations?

I build small prototypes and internal tools with n8n, APIs, RAG and LLM document checks, like the GST invoice-check demo above. I'm not offering production-grade AI systems, and I'll tell you plainly if your project needs one.
