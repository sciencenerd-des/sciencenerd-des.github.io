08 / Frequently asked questions

Common questions before we start

Should I choose Excel, Power BI, or Tableau?

Pick Excel for costing, reconciliation, and operational workbooks your team edits directly; Power BI for Microsoft 365 teams needing automated data models; and Tableau for visual exploration and executive storytelling. If you are unsure, I recommend the best fit during scoping.

What do you need from me to give a fixed-price quote?

Share the decisions you want to make, a small anonymized sample of your exports or current sheet, and your preferred tool or deadline. I reply with a clear written scope, timeline, and fixed price.

Can you work with Tally, Zoho Books, Shopify, or marketplace exports?

Yes. Most client projects begin with raw CSV or Excel exports from Tally, Zoho Books, Shopify, Amazon or Flipkart marketplaces, or SQL databases, cleaned into repeatable, documented tables.

How does fixed pricing work for India vs. international clients?

International projects are quoted in USD ($100+ audits and cleanups, $200–$500 single dashboards, $500–$1,000 multi-page models, or $40/hour). Indian clients can be invoiced in rupees (from ₹8,000 for audits and cleanups, about ₹35,000 per project, or about ₹4,000/hour for open-ended work).

How fast will I see results?

Audits and cleanups usually wrap up in 2–4 days. Single dashboards deliver a working draft by Day 5 and complete end-to-end in roughly 2 weeks, including a revision round and walkthrough.

Can I hire you through Upwork or Freelancer?

Yes—you can work directly via email and invoice, or hire through my verified Upwork or Freelancer profiles if you prefer platform escrow.

Do you also build AI document checks or automation prototypes?

Yes. On request, I build targeted automation prototypes using Python, n8n, APIs, RAG, and LLM-assisted document checks (like GST invoice validation paired with deterministic rules), quoted per job.
